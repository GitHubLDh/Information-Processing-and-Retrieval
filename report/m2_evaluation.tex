\section{Evaluation}
\label{sec:evaluation}

We evaluate both setups on the five information needs. Because no public qrels
exist for this collection, we pool the top-20 results from Setup A and Setup B for
each need, deduplicate by \texttt{movie\_id}, and judge every pooled movie on a
three-point scale: 0 (not relevant), 1 (partially relevant), and 2 (highly
relevant). Judgments are recorded in \texttt{eval/qrels.csv}. We report precision
at 10 (P@10), average precision and mean average precision (AP/MAP), nDCG@10, and
precision--recall curves, comparing Setup A and Setup B per information need and
overall. The protocol is fixed before judging, and a second person spot-checks a
sample of the judgments. Relevance judgments and final metrics are added once the
pooled results have been judged; this section currently describes the protocol
only.
